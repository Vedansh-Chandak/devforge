## Type-check gotchas fixed in this phase (strict `tsc --noEmit` on integration-tests)
- `ArtifactKind` union has no `'SRC'` — use `'FILE'`.
- `RepairStrategy` union lacks `'MODIFY'` — use `'REWRITE'` + required `scope: 'BROAD'|'MINIMAL'`.
- `pool.register` wants `RoleAgent` (`run` → `Promise<TaskResult>`), not a raw `AgentBackend`; wrap with `outputToResult(task, await backend(task, ctx), ctx, 1)`.
- `CommandResult.command` is a union `Command`; cast via `as CommandResult['command']` for arbitrary fixtures.
